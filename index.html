Mosquito control model lab

Change the growth rate, carrying capacity and control effort, and watch all four graphs for Project 1 update together. The starting values come from a logistic fit to 2017 San Diego County trap counts: r = 1.48 per month and K = 61 mosquitoes per trap visit.

dP/dt = rP(1 − P/K) − h
Data: County of San Diego Department of Environmental Health, Vector Control Program, Routine Mosquito Surveillance Traps (CivicData), 2015–2018. Time is in months; P is the mean mosquitoes per trap visit.
